2 John is a postcard to the elect lady and her children — a church, or a woman and her household, loved in truth. It is the New Testament at its shortest-but-one, kept in the Protestant canon because truth and love had to share a page, and because hospitality was how teachers spread. It belongs as a line drawn at the door.

The elder to the elect lady. Grace, mercy, and peace in truth and love. I rejoiced to find some of your children walking in the truth. Love one another. This is love: that we walk according to his commandments. The opening is affection with a spine. The commandment is not new.

Many deceivers have gone out into the world who do not confess Jesus Christ coming in the flesh. Such a one is the deceiver and the antichrist. Watch yourselves. If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into your house or give him any greeting, lest you share in his wicked works. Hospitality was how teachers spread. The letter is drawing a line at the threshold.

I have much to write to you, but I would rather not use paper and ink. I hope to come and talk face to face, so that our joy may be complete. The children of your elect sister greet you. What is left open is the visit, and whether the door will hold. Truth and love in one page, with a sharp edge on the threshold.

Read it with 1 John, not as a free-standing rule about visitors. Hard parts are obvious: the closed door, the withheld greeting, a line later churches have used against the wrong people. Famous verses need their setting: love one another is walking in the commandments; the deceiver is named by a doctrine — Jesus Christ coming in the flesh — not by manners. Face to face is the elder’s preference. Paper is the emergency.

1The elder, to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth, and not I only, but also all those who know the truth, 2for the truth’s sake, which remains in us, and it will be with us forever: 3Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us, from God the Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.

4I rejoice greatly that I have found some of your children walking in truth, even as we have been commanded by the Father. 5Now I beg you, dear lady, not as though I wrote to you a new commandment, but that which we had from the beginning, that we love one another. 6This is love, that we should walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, even as you heard from the beginning, that you should walk in it. 7For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who don’t confess that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. This is the deceiver and the Antichrist. 8Watch yourselves, that we don’t lose the things which we have accomplished, but that we receive a full reward. 9Whoever transgresses and doesn’t remain in the teaching of Christ, doesn’t have God. He who remains in the teaching has both the Father and the Son. 10If anyone comes to you, and doesn’t bring this teaching, don’t receive him into your house, and don’t welcome him, 11for he who welcomes him participates in his evil deeds.

12Having many things to write to you, I don’t want to do so with paper and ink, but I hope to come to you, and to speak face to face, that our joy may be made full. 13The children of your chosen sister greet you. Amen.
