Hosea prophesies to the northern kingdom in the last decades before Samaria falls to Assyria, and his marriage is the sermon. It sits among the Prophets because the Lord’s word is aimed at a people’s worship and politics, and because the prophet’s household is made to carry the message. Go, take to yourself a wife of whoredom, for the land commits great whoredom by forsaking the Lord. Gomer. Children named Jezreel — the valley of Jehu’s blood — then No Mercy, then Not My People. Israel has chased other gods like a lover who will not stay. God is not done: I will hedge her way with thorns; I will allure her into the wilderness and speak to her heart; I will say you are my people, and she shall say you are my God.

The opening movement is family as oracle. Go again, love a woman who is loved by another and is an adulteress, even as the Lord loves the children of Israel. Buy her back. She shall remain many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or pillar, without ephod or household gods. Afterward the children of Israel shall return and seek the Lord their God and David their king. The names will be reversed: mercy on No Mercy; to Not My People, you are my people. The first three chapters are the whole book in miniature: indictment, tenderness, a future that has to be bought.

The rest is lawsuit and tenderness braided. There is no faithfulness or steadfast love or knowledge of God in the land — only swearing, lying, murder, stealing, and adultery. My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge. A spirit of whoredom. Priests and kings are named. Hear this, O priests. Come, let us return to the Lord; he has torn, and he will heal; he has struck, and he will bind us up. After two days he will revive us. I desire steadfast love and not sacrifice, the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings — Jesus will quote that, twice. Ephraim mixes himself with the peoples; he is a cake not turned. They sow the wind and reap the whirlwind. When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called my son. How can I give you up, O Ephraim? How can I hand you over, O Israel? I am God and not a man, the Holy One in your midst, and I will not come in wrath.

The last chapter is a plea and a promise. Return, O Israel, to the Lord your God, for you have stumbled because of your iniquity. Take with you words and return: take away all iniquity; accept that which is good. Assyria will not save us; we will not ride on horses; we will not say to the work of our hands, you are our gods. I will heal their apostasy; I will love them freely, for my anger has turned from them. I will be as the dew to Israel; he shall blossom like the lily, take root like Lebanon. From me comes your fruit. Whoever is wise, let him understand these things. What is left hanging is Ephraim’s answer. Samaria will fall. The names No Mercy and Not My People hang over a people who have not yet said you are my God.

Read Hosea as stubborn love, not as an excuse to linger on a woman’s shame. The hard part is the sexual metaphor: whoredom, purchase, children named for judgment. It is aimed at Israel’s worship of Baal and at alliances with Assyria and Egypt, not at a licence to despise Gomer. Judgment is real; so is the refusal to quit. Paul will quote Not My People in Romans as a door for the nations. Matthew will hear out of Egypt I called my son. The book ends with a plea to return, and a promise that the Lord will heal their apostasy and love them freely. Do not resolve the braid. The lawsuit and the lullaby are the same voice.

1Yahweh’s word that came to Hosea the son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash, king of Israel. 2When Yahweh spoke at first by Hosea, Yahweh said to Hosea, “Go, take for yourself a wife of prostitution and children of unfaithfulness; for the land commits great adultery, forsaking Yahweh.”

3So he went and took Gomer the daughter of Diblaim; and she conceived, and bore him a son.

4Yahweh said to him, “Call his name Jezreel; for yet a little while, and I will avenge the blood of Jezreel on the house of Jehu, and will cause the kingdom of the house of Israel to cease. 5It will happen in that day that I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel.”

6She conceived again, and bore a daughter.

6Then he said to him, “Call her name Lo-Ruhamah; for I will no longer have mercy on the house of Israel, that I should in any way pardon them. 7But I will have mercy on the house of Judah, and will save them by Yahweh their God, and will not save them by bow, sword, battle, horses, or horsemen.”

8Now when she had weaned Lo-Ruhamah, she conceived, and bore a son.

9He said, “Call his name Lo-Ammi; for you are not my people, and I will not be yours. 10Yet the number of the children of Israel will be as the sand of the sea, which can’t be measured or counted; and it will come to pass that, in the place where it was said to them, ‘You are not my people,’ they will be called ‘sons of the living God.’ 11The children of Judah and the children of Israel will be gathered together, and they will appoint themselves one head, and will go up from the land; for great will be the day of Jezreel.

and the prophet will also stumble with you in the night;
and I will destroy your mother.
6My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge.
Because you have rejected knowledge, I will also reject you,
that you may be no priest to me.
Because you have forgotten your God’s law,
I will also forget your children.
7As they were multiplied, so they sinned against me.
I will change their glory into shame.
8They feed on the sin of my people,
and set their heart on their iniquity.
9It will be, like people, like priest;
and I will punish them for their ways,
and will repay them for their deeds.
10They will eat, and not have enough.
They will play the prostitute, and will not increase;
because they have abandoned giving to Yahweh.
11Prostitution, wine, and new wine take away understanding.
12My people consult with their wooden idol,
and answer to a stick of wood.
Indeed the spirit of prostitution has led them astray,
and they have been unfaithful to their God.
13They sacrifice on the tops of the mountains,
and burn incense on the hills, under oaks and poplars and terebinths,
because its shade is good.
Therefore your daughters play the prostitute,
and your brides commit adultery.

nor your brides when they commit adultery;
because the men consort with prostitutes,

17Ephraim is joined to idols.
Leave him alone!
18Their drink has become sour.
They play the prostitute continually.
Her rulers dearly love their shameful way.
19The wind has wrapped her up in its wings;
