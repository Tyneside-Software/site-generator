Addendum 2. Experience, Therefore, Reform This ontology stands upon the shoulders of the greatest thinkers of the past. From Descartes to Einstein, from Martin Luther to Spinoza. Human beings much smarter than I who also wrestled with their minds and conscience to make sense of experience. This book exists as a testimony to their lives and work. In the same vein, this ontology will live on through the lives and work of the many people who will come after to unpack, expand, and build upon it. This ontology is not closed, it is the replacing of doubt with the tool of certainty. A tool that is united to the preference of building, not tearing down. I experience, therefore I AM. Everything that derives from that experience is subjective. These words do not objectively exist in this form, you may be simulating it for all you know. But your experience of this moment certainly does exist. The call of the Reformation that provides the intellectual foundation of the Western World has been both the call to return and equally the call to rebuild. Always Reforming. Semper Reformanda. Reform, therefore.
