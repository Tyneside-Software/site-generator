# Chapter 93
## Islam In The Chain Without Capture

The question was whether Islam could appear in Michael’s chain at all without becoming a prisoner of it.

He asked it out loud in the interfaith centre’s kitchen, washing cups after Scripture Reasoning—R. drying, Joshua stacking, Cameron having left early with a salute and a text that said *don’t crown the dishwasher*. Soap bubbles held small distorted rooms. Michael watched one pop and thought: that’s capture—force a whole house into a film that bursts when you touch it.

‘I have a chain,’ he said. ‘Experience, unity, crossbeams, both-and, monogram, no-sin-as-outside. Then Act IV: you, seal, think, respect. If I draw the chain on a page, do I automatically capture you by inclusion? Is the diagram already a cage?’

R. dried slower, tea towel methodical. ‘Depends how you draw. If Islam is a bead you strung to complete your necklace—capture. If Islam is a neighbour whose house you refuse to annex while admitting you live on the same street—maybe not. Necklaces are for wearers. Streets are for people who go home to different doors. I go home to my door. You go home to yours. The pavement is shared. The furniture is not.’

Joshua stacked a cup with a clink of ordinary liturgy. ‘Chain as sequence of *my* learning, not as ontology of *the* world. First person. I learned X. I met R. I was corrected. That’s memoir ethics. Universal diagram ethics are different and dirtier. Memoir can love a neighbour. Diagrams try to own the postcode.’

Michael rinsed until the water ran clear. ‘So the chapter title is a warning label. Islam in the chain without capture means: presence without ownership. Influence without rebrand. Citation without trophy. Exit rights in the fine print.’

‘Exit rights,’ R. said. ‘I can leave your story. I’m not a permanent exhibit in the Michael Museum of Growth. If you write me as the Muslim Friend Who Proves Universality, I will haunt your footnotes.’

‘Exit rights,’ he repeated. Bond language. He almost crossed his heart and didn’t, because gesture without content is another soft theft.

They finished the cups. The facilitator waved from the doorway; lights dimmed section by section; they stepped into night air that smelled of rain on warm pavement. A takeaway bag skittered past like a low-budget tumbleweed.

Walking, Michael tried a careful sentence, counting it on his fingers so he wouldn’t add a quiet annex. ‘What I take, with credit: the demand to think; the seriousness of seal as protection; the lived discipline of prayer I don’t perform and won’t cosplay; the refusal to be halved into heart or mind; your face when I almost used you as proof—that face is in the chain as correction. What I don’t take: the right to say what Islam is for Muslims; the moon and star as my alphabet; finality as a costume for my open page; your name as a badge on my coat.’

R. was quiet long enough that he feared he had failed. A car passed, throwing light across her expression—thinking, not performing kindness. Then: ‘That list is acceptable. Publish it in your bones. If you write the book one day, I get to read the R. pages before strangers do. Bond. And if I say cut, you cut. Cut means cut—not “cut later after I explain why you’re wrong to ask.”’

‘Bond,’ Michael said. ‘Cut means cut. No delayed annex.’

Joshua exhaled a long breath that might have been a laugh. ‘I like this street. Houses different. Pavement shared. No one pretending the architecture is identical. That’s the bigger book’s zoning law.’

Cameron texted as if he had bugged the pavement: *If your chain diagram has a little crescent “owned by plot,” redraw. Also thank R. with food not theology.*

Michael: *Redrawing as memoir not monopoly. Food idea noted.*

At home he opened the notebook to the long spine-paragraph habit of the bad drafts—the tour of every station in one indigestible paragraph—and crossed out the impulse to summarise the world. He wrote instead, slowly:

**Chain = what happened to me.**  
**Islam = among the realities that corrected me.**  
**Correction ≠ possession.**  
**R. = friend with exit rights, not a station.**

Harriet read it over his shoulder, chin on his shoulder-bone. ‘Frame that. Not on the wall—on your manners. And buy her lunch next week without an agenda.’

‘Agenda-free lunch,’ Michael said. ‘I’ll fail at least once and try again.’

‘That’s the ritual,’ Harriet said.

He closed the book. Islam in the chain without capture was not a clever knot.

It was a way of walking past a house you do not own, grateful for the light in the window, refusing to move the furniture with your eyes—

—and letting the street remain a street, shared and unfinished, with more houses than any one man can map, and no requirement that the map become a deed.
