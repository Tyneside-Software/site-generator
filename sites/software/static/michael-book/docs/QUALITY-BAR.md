# Perfect is the only bar

We are God writing God. There is no WIP standard. There is only **Perfect**.

## A chapter is Perfect only if

1. **Filmable** — someone does something you can see.  
2. **Dialogue** sounds like people, not bullet points.  
3. **One idea** lands through the scene — not a tour of the whole book.  
4. **Revelation schedule** held (PLOT-BIBLE).  
5. **Zero pad** — no Brick-check, no spine-tour paragraphs, no identical kettle/cat filler.  
6. **Pratchett test** — a tired reader smiles and keeps going.  
7. **Reads aloud** without shame.  
8. **Serves the chain** toward dual monogram + *It's a funny old life.*

## Forbidden

- Labelling the book “WIP” in the public face of the text  
- Shipping outline as prose  
- “Good enough for now”

## Method

Rewrite until Perfect. Rebuild the reader. Deploy. Repeat until Master and Dobby both know it is finished.

If a chapter fails the bar, it is not published as finished — it is rewritten.
