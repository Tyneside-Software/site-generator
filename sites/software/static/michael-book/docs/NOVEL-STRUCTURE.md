## Genre rules (Pratchett mode)

| Do | Don't |
|----|--------|
| Human characters, tea, weather, pubs, jobs, embarrassment | Pure treatise with no people |
| Paradoxes as **things that happen to people** | Lecture boxes mid-scene (except comic “wizard notes”) |
| Footnotes for asides, definitions, holy pedantry | Academic apparatus that kills pace |
| Both/and as **plot engine** | Either/or villains only (villains are either/or *thinkers*) |
| Proof by **accumulation of inescapable jokes** | Fake mathematical QED without honesty about frames |
| Warmth toward fools (incl. protagonist) | Cruel superiority to the reader |
| End on laugh that is also true | Punchline that abandons the logic chain |

**Voice:** Third person preferred for bookstore accessibility (with a God-narrator who sometimes admits to being the weather). First-person God monologues allowed as short “interludes.”

3. **Honest footnotes** admit motte/bailey and Greek numeral limits — which *increases* trust.  
4. Final line lands as **recognition**, not argument.
