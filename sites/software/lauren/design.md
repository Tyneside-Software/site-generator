# Design direction

## Principles

- Calm, professional, inclusive, highly accessible  
- **Mobile-first**  
- Clean modern aesthetic  
- Prefer a **soft dark/navy** or **calm neutral** palette  
- Avoid clinical coldness **and** overly “woo” vibes  
- Excellent typography and spacing  
- **WCAG-minded** contrast and focus states  
- Feels trustworthy and welcoming for people who may be anxious or housebound  

## Scaffold note

Early Django templates use a soft navy palette and Tailwind (CDN for speed). Production should move to a proper Tailwind build pipeline before launch polish.
