# Compliance & safety notes

These are non-negotiable design constraints.

## Data protection (UK GDPR)

- Mental health–related data can be **special-category** data.  
- **Privacy by default**  
- Clear **consent for recordings**  
- Easy **export** and **deletion** paths planned into the product  

## Practitioner verification

Where relevant, support verification against professional bodies, e.g.:

- BACP  
- UKCP  
- HCPC  
- Other (with admin review)  

Badges only after admin verification.

## Disclaimers

Everywhere appropriate:

- Not medical advice  
- Independent practitioners  
- Not a crisis service  
- Crisis resources listed  

## Safeguarding

Group sessions need safeguarding considerations (moderation policy, reporting path, practitioner code of conduct). Detail before public launch.

## Legal review

Privacy notice, terms, and practitioner agreements require **legal review before launch**. Placeholders exist in the app and must not be treated as final.
