# House rendering — Douay-Rheims, modern English

We do **not** republish NIV, NLT, or ESV.

Source: public-domain **Douay-Rheims (Challoner / 1899 American)**.
Output: our own accessible modern English, verse for verse, accurate to that source.

## Locked readings

`genesis-1.txt` and `revelation-22.txt` are Master’s own wording. Never overwrite them.

Treat those two as the style examples for every other chapter:

- Modern English, still close to the Douay-Rheims (firmament, “And so it was done”, “the Word was made flesh”).
- Keep verse numbers and verse count.
- Do not flatten names (Jonas, Ninive, Isaias) unless the locked samples already modernise that name.
- British spelling where it appears in the samples (*practises*).
- God as “he” after the first mention, as in Genesis 1.
- Revelation is titled **Apocalypse (Revelation)** on the page.

## Files

`{stem}-{chapter}.txt` — one verse per line: `1 In the beginning…`

First lines may be comments starting with `#`.

The published site includes **every chapter** of the Catholic Douay-Rheims canon. Chapters without a file here are served as Challoner and labelled “Douay-Rheims” until a house file exists.
