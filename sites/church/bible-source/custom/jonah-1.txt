# Jonah 1
# Modern English rendering of the Douay-Rheims
1 Now the word of the Lord came to Jonah the son of Amittai, saying:
2 Arise and go to Nineveh, that great city, and preach in it, for its wickedness has come up before me.
3 But Jonah rose up to flee to Tarshish from the face of the Lord. He went down to Joppa and found a ship going to Tarshish. So he paid the fare and went down into it to go with them to Tarshish from the face of the Lord.
4 But the Lord sent a great wind upon the sea, and a mighty tempest was raised on the sea, so that the ship was in danger of being broken.
5 The mariners were afraid, and each cried out to his god. They threw the cargo that was in the ship into the sea to lighten it. But Jonah had gone down into the inner part of the ship and was fast asleep.
6 The shipmaster came to him and said, “Why are you fast asleep? Rise up, call upon your God, if perhaps God will think of us so that we may not perish.”
7 And they said to one another, “Come, let us cast lots, that we may know why this evil has come upon us.” So they cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.
8 They said to him, “Tell us why this evil has come upon us. What is your business? Where do you come from? Where are you going? Of what people are you?”
9 He said to them, “I am a Hebrew, and I fear the Lord, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land.”
10 The men were greatly afraid and said to him, “Why have you done this?” (For the men knew that he was fleeing from the face of the Lord, because he had told them.)
11 They said to him, “What shall we do to you, that the sea may calm down for us?” For the sea was growing more and more tempestuous.
12 He said to them, “Take me up and cast me into the sea, and the sea will calm down for you. For I know that this great tempest has come upon you because of me.”
13 The men rowed hard to return to land, but they could not, because the sea grew more and more violent against them.
14 They cried out to the Lord and said, “We beseech you, O Lord, let us not perish for this man’s life, and do not lay innocent blood on us, for you, O Lord, have done as it pleased you.”
15 They took Jonah and cast him into the sea, and the sea ceased from its raging.
16 The men feared the Lord exceedingly, and they offered a sacrifice to the Lord and made vows.
