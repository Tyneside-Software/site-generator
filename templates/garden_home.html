Gardens, not climate units

Mowing, beds, hedges, weeding, the path, the outside that gets ignored. Ordinary work that makes a house liveable. If you want lifetime carbon and Greenacres, that is tyneside.green.

Start from work that exists

The first version is not a recruitment drive. Someone already looks after Michael's house. Asking that person whether gardens are in scope is the practical next step — then a handful of nearby jobs, not a national brand.

Same honesty as cleaning

Pay the person who does the work. Don’t invent a platform first. If booking software helps later, it can sit beside cleaning WhatsApp booking. None of that is built for garden yet.

What this is not (yet)

Not a shop window of prices

There is no £X per hour on this page because we have not set one in public. A quote would be a conversation, not a catalogue.

Not landscape architecture

The sketch is maintenance and keeping places decent — the outdoor cousin of a 2-hour clean — not show gardens or design-and-build. That can change if the work does. Today it has not.
