Looking at Greenacres’ BNG pages, a number in the region of £25,000 jumped out — the same order of money as covering a lifetime of personal carbon. That is not a corporate fantasy. It is a sum a person can decide to find. This page is the project that follows from that thought.

Honest status — August 2026

An idea. No money has moved. No conversation with Greenacres yet. No Gift Aid (Tyneside Charity is not a registered charity yet). No volunteer days booked. The next useful human step is making contact at Greenacres.

Two speeds, then a stretch

The easy path should not wait for volunteers, software, or a charity number. The further path should not be blocked just because someone only has money, or only has time.

01 · Easy

Pay Greenacres directly

They already sell what this needs: biodiversity units, and through Green Acres Future carbon / tree-planting options, from land in Stannington, Morpeth, Northumberland. A person or a family could pay them. Tyneside would be a signpost, not a shop.

02 · Further

Show up and plant

Money buys habitat. Hands go further. Volunteer tree planting — if Greenacres (or a neighbour project) will have us — is how this becomes more than a transfer. Same honesty as the rest of Tyneside: real hours, not a slogan.

03 · Stretch

Route through charity

The idea is to send gifts via tyneside.charity so Gift Aid adds 25p for every £1 from HMRC — £25,000 becomes £31,250, at no extra cost to a basic-rate taxpayer. That only works once the programme is a registered charity. It is not. Today, charity donations fund free cleans through Tyneside Cleaning. We will not pretend otherwise.

This is a sketch number, not a quote

Biodiversity Net Gain units (what developers buy so a site leaves nature at least 10% better) often cost in the mid tens of thousands of pounds per unit — Greenacres’ own understanding BNG page is where this started. A UK person’s carbon is a different unit: on the order of several tonnes of CO2e a year, so a lifetime is hundreds of tonnes. At ordinary offset prices that lands in the same tens of thousands, not millions.

BNG units are not carbon tonnes. We are not selling either. The point of the number is moral and practical: if a lifetime can be faced at the scale of a house deposit or a used car, then a Tyneside door that helps people actually do it is worth sketching. Greenacres can tell us the real menu when we talk to them.

Greenacres — local, not invented here

We did not start a habitat bank. There is already one up the A1. The honest first version of Tyneside Green is to use it, then add people and Gift Aid if we can.

Who they are (public facts)

Greenacres BNG supplies off-site biodiversity units from Northumberland. Sister company Green Acres Future offers carbon, trees, and related work. Address on their site: Green Acres, Stannington, Morpeth, NE61 6EF. BNG enquiries: 01670 611001.

We are not their partner, reseller, or agent. Naming them is the plan, not a claim of a deal.

What we owe them next

A conversation. What does a lifetime of personal carbon actually cost on their menu? Can a private individual buy, or is BNG developer-only? Would volunteer planting days be welcome? Could later charity purchases sit alongside their community / CSR work?

Until that call happens, everything on this page is our reading of their public site plus an idea. If they would rather we phrased any of this differently, we will.

Source we started from: greenacresbng.co.uk/understanding-bng · Gift Aid rule: GOV.UK Gift Aid (25p per £1, registered charities only).

Why tyneside.charity is in the picture

Direct payment to Greenacres is clean and available without us. Routing through tyneside.charity is the stretch: same gift, more habitat, if Gift Aid applies. That is 25p extra per £1 from HMRC — and only if the donor has paid enough tax to cover it.

Today that stretch is closed. The welcome-home cleans programme is not registered with the Charity Commission, so it cannot claim Gift Aid. We already say so on the charity site. When and if that changes, Green can use the same pot without inventing a second story.

What this is not

Not a live checkout

There is no “offset my life” button here. If you want to act today, talk to Greenacres (or plant with someone who will have you). Tell us if you do — we would like to learn in public.

Not a licence to emit

Paying for habitat does not make a lifetime of flying and driving fine. The sketch is: some people will still want a number they can finish. £25k-scale makes that a real choice instead of a shrug.

Where it sits in the family

Lives on the group sketchbook until it earns a homepage door. Charity is the stretch. Cleaning remains the indoor jobs loop. Paid gardening is a different door: tyneside.garden.
