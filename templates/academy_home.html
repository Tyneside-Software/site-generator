Two things we could do with this

Either, both, or neither. Written down so the idea doesn’t live only in chat.

A · The notes

Do something with Lewis’s site

He is already writing down what he learns so it isn’t trapped in a folder. That could stay private. Or it could become a public, living revision site — a Tyneside-shaped set of notes other early-takers (and ordinary-year pupils) could actually use. His voice, his gaps, his worked examples. Not a content farm. A trail of one person going through the spec.

Nothing from that site is on this page yet. When he is ready to show it, it can live here.

B · Other kids

Tutor more people, maybe as a group

If the method works for Lewis, it might work for others — paid sessions, perhaps a small group rather than only one-to-one. Same honesty as cleaning: start with work we can actually do, Howden / Tyneside first, no pretend campus.

No price list. No subjects advertised. No DBS claim on this page because we are not taking bookings. If a parent WhatsApps, we will say where we actually are — likely “not yet”.

What this is not

Not the school proposal

One day a month in the real company, with Mr Merrit and the school team, lives at tyneside.software/school.html. That is about Lewis at work. Academy is about exams and notes. Related son. Different door.

Not a school

No classrooms, no Ofsted, no “academy chain”. The word on the domain is the ambition, not a status. Safeguarding, qualifications to teach, and whether group tuition even happens — all still open.

Where it sits

On the group sketchbook until it earns a homepage door. Games is Lewis’s proof of shipping. Software is the jobs engine. Academy would be the learning door if the notes — or the tutoring — grow up.
