Then the useful fact: a friend owns Dynamite Valley in Ponsanooth, Cornwall — real ale under the viaduct, golden ales to IPAs, bottles, cans, cask and keg. The idea: order through this door, they brew and ship, and 2% goes to tyneside.charity — same pattern as tyneside.store and RST. Not mashed on the Tyne. We’re a sociable bunch.

DV Dynamite Valley gets the actual order

1. They brew it

Dynamite Valley — Ponsanooth, under the viaduct, Viaduct Works, Frog Hill, TR3 7JW. A friend’s brewery. Stock stays with them. No Tyneside fermenter.

2. You order through Tyneside

Pay Dynamite Valley’s price + 2% on this door. Example: their crate £10.00 → you pay £10.20 (£10.00 to the brewery · £0.20 charity). Same arithmetic as tyneside.store and RST.

3. The order goes to them

Dropshipping: Dynamite Valley receive the order and ship the beer. We tell the truth on the page: brewed in Cornwall, enjoyed on Tyneside, a sliver to Howden welcome-home cleans.

That 2% is the point of ordering here

If you only wanted the beer, you could buy from dynamitevalley.co.uk today. Coming through this door is how the extra 2% reaches tyneside.charity. That only works when we take the payment — same honesty as the store: a click-through to the brewery does not collect the 2%. And Gift Aid only exists once that charity is registered. It is not.

Not produced in Tyneside. That’s ok.

The family is jobs, care, kit, practice, and a few doors that started as jokes. Beer is the sociable one. We do not need a mash tun on the Tyne to raise a glass with people who live here. We do need to say where it was made.

If Dynamite Valley would rather we didn’t put their name on a sketch, we will take it off. This is an open plan, not a surprise marketing campaign.

Why there is no “buy now”

Alcohol is licensed

Selling beer online in England is not a Shopify theme. You generally need the right premises licence at the place the drink is dispatched (and a personal licence / designated premises supervisor in the usual way). Dropshipping does not magic that away. We have not done that work. So there is no checkout.

If this ever becomes a shop it will be for adults only. Until then: drink Dynamite Valley from them if you want a pint today — dynamitevalley.co.uk — and WhatsApp us if you want to help turn the joke into a tidy little door.
